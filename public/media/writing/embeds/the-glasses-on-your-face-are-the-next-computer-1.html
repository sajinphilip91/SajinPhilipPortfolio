Try GlassScore on your Meta glasses

GlassScore is built as a Web App for Meta Ray-Ban Display, so you can load it directly onto your glasses in a couple of minutes — no app store required.

meta-glass-scores.vercel.app Open Link
  1. Check your glasses are compatible

    Web Apps currently run only on Meta Ray-Ban Display, with the in-lens screen. Make sure your glasses software is on v125 or newer (Meta AI app → Devices → gear icon → General → About).

  2. Update the Meta AI app

    You'll need version v272 or later. Check this under Meta AI app → Settings → App Info, and update from the App Store or Play Store if needed.

  3. Turn on Developer Mode

    In the Meta AI app, go to Settings → App Info, then tap the App version number five times. A pop-up will appear — tap Enable. This only needs to be done once.

  4. Add GlassScore as a Web App

    Go to App Settings → App Connections → Web Apps → Add a Web App. Give it a name (e.g. "GlassScore"), paste in meta-glass-scores.vercel.app, and tap Connect.

  5. Launch it on your glasses

    GlassScore will appear in your Meta Ray-Ban Display app grid right away. Select it to open the live football and cricket scores on your lens, and pin it for quick access next time.

Tip: Once inside GlassScore, use up/down/left/right swipes and a pinch or tap to navigate. A middle pinch brings up the menu to restart, resume, or manage permissions — useful if you want to re-enable voice control.
