An interesting move by #Rapido.

You can set your fare for your travel.

<figure><img src="/media/writing/09349e-4804c68c80ed4aa2960c6a4010284b2a.webp" alt="" width="1332" height="772" loading="lazy"></figure>

Yes, It seems like Rapido has indeed introduced an innovative feature where customers can choose their own fare for the trip. This approach could potentially offer more flexibility and control over the cost of the ride to the user.

It’s similar to the concept of tipping, where a higher tip might encourage faster service or a better experience. By allowing users to set their own fare, it might also create a competitive environment where drivers are more likely to accept rides from those who are willing to pay a bit more, especially during peak hours or in high-demand areas.

This could be a strategic move to balance supply and demand, ensuring that customers who need a ride urgently can get one by offering a higher fare. This is the same concept of "Tip to Drivet" which they are introduced earlier.

The change in the user interface and the copy alterations are likely aimed at making this new pricing model clear and user-friendly. It’s an interesting development in the ride-sharing economy, reflecting a shift towards more dynamic pricing models that can adapt to real-time conditions and customer preferences.
