Have you ever come across a 404 error page on Amazon's website? I did, and it was an unexpectedly delightful experience! The error message was accompanied by a cute dog image and a caption that read "Milly - Meet the Dogs of Amazon". Curious, I clicked on the link and was taken to a wonderful page that talked about how Amazon treats its pets.

<figure><img src="/media/writing/09349e-86f9999bbd0c4e7b8a3891696bb43080.webp" alt="" width="1332" height="772" loading="lazy"></figure>

More than 10,000 dogs are registered to come to work at Amazon, and even more amazing pets make cameo appearances on video calls and in companywide chat groups. Says on Amazon page.

Research has shown that pets can support a healthier response to stress, lowering blood pressure and resting heart rate. And according to researchers in Japan, a dog's gaze can raise levels of the mood-boosting hormone oxytocin. I recently discovered this, and I must give huge applause to Amazon for this initiative. It's refreshing to see a company that values the happiness and health of its employees and their pets.
