In this modern world where most of us shop online, it’s easy to get lost in product specs we can’t visualize.

Ever tried to figure out how big a 45MM watch dial really is? Feels abstract, right?

That’s why I loved this small but smart touch on AJIO — they placed the watch next to a credit card. Simple, familiar, and instantly relatable. No need to Google measurements or hunt for a scale.

It’s a great reminder that good UX isn’t always about big features. Sometimes, it's about tiny decisions that remove friction and make things feel obvious.
