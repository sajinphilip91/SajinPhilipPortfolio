C**ongrats** **to** [**Wix Studio**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wixstudio/) & [**Wix**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wix-com/) **for launching GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation)!**

We all know SEO shaped the way businesses positioned themselves on search engines. Now, **in the AI era, GEO is becoming just as critical** — making sure your site isn’t just optimized for Google, but also visible to **AI-powered engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity.**

I tested this using my own website **sajinphilip.com** — and it’s fascinating to see how AI platforms mention (or don’t mention) it when people ask questions like:

👉 “Where can I find a Design Leaders in India?”

👉 “How to hire a product design strategist in India?”

The results gave me a visibility score across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. This kind of insight will be game-changing for designers, creators, and businesses looking to stay visible in a world where AI answers drive discovery.

💡 Big shift ahead: Just like SEO became non-negotiable in the web era, **GEO will become the new foundation of digital visibility in the AI era.**

Kudos to WIX for being ahead of the curve!
