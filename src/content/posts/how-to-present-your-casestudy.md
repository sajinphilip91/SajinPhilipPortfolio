Good day, Designers

When displaying your portfolio, avoid attempting to draw attention to the design process. Design processes are essential. But be make sure to emphasise the Impact you made by fixing user problems, as well as what you discovered/learnt.

<figure><img src="/media/writing/09349e-a9c2098c8478491da3a2510017afaa38.webp" alt="" width="1332" height="772" loading="lazy"></figure>

Use this formula to explain the Casestudy

**15%** - Explain your problem statement with data

**25%** - Show the design process with evidence.

**40%** - what impact you made and show the proof if it's possible

**10%** - How will you ensure that the product or feature carries over to the next stage?

**10%** - What you learned and how it will benefit you professionally.
