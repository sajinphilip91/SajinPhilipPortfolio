Great news from LinkedIn! 🎉

Have you ever hesitated to apply for a job on LinkedIn because the post showed that 800 others had already applied? It can be quite discouraging, making you wonder if it’s even worth sending your CV.

<figure><img src="/media/writing/09349e-84566133b3284b438ea5ac12a5767050.webp" alt="" width="1332" height="772" loading="lazy"></figure>

To address this, LinkedIn initially changed the display to “100+ applicants.” However, this still left users guessing—was it 101 or 900? Now, LinkedIn has made a brilliant move by showing “12 Potential applicants” instead of just “100+ applicants.”

This subtle change significantly impacts Human behaviour and motivation. Knowing that you are one of the relevant applicants can boost your confidence and encourage you to apply.

This is a perfect example of how a small adjustment can lead to a major positive shift in user behaviour and core business success. It's fascinating to witness how a minor modification in presentation can lead to a major positive change in Human behaviour and ultimately benefit the core business. Kudos to LinkedIn for this innovative approach!
