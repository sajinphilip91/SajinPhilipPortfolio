[Netflix](https://www.linkedin.com/company/netflix/) has done a great job designing the "Download Information" screen. There is plenty of information to help the user make better decisions.

As a user, you don't think much about making decisions on this screen. Most of the information that you need to proceed with is already listed on the screen.

I love the way that Netflix showcases the remaining storage space and how much space the new download will take.<br>

<figure><img src="/media/writing/09349e-af062cd2a31a4e71bf74036f5285b37b.webp" alt="" width="1062" height="869" loading="lazy"></figure>

A 3GB download is approximately 12 hours of movies. How amazing to see this information! I was travelling almost the entire night, and it helped me determine how much I needed to download the video content on my phone.

Kudos, [#Netflix](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/hashtag/?keywords=netflix&highlightedUpdateUrns=urn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A7123629041298898944) team!
