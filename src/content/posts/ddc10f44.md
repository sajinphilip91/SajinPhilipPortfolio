On June 28, 2025, the European Accessibility Act (EAA) comes into full effect – a major milestone for accessibility, inclusion, and business across the EU.

The EAA is landmark legislation that requires many products and services—think websites, apps, e-commerce platforms, ATMs, transport booking systems, banking, and even e-books—to be accessible for people with disabilities and the elderly.

**Why does the EAA matter?**

- Empowers 100+ million Europeans living with disabilities, and benefits ALL users by making digital and physical products easier to use.
- Levels the playing field for businesses by harmonizing accessibility requirements across the EU, helping organizations trade smoothly and innovate for a wider market.
- Drives a culture of inclusion by making accessibility a legal requirement in both public and private sectors—not just a 'nice to have'

**Here’s what’s changing:**

✅ Mandatory accessibility for digital goods & services

✅ Legal risks for non-compliance
